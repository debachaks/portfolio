Abstract

I'm a PhD student in Computer Engineering at Arizona State University, advised by Dr. Eirini Eleni Tsiropoulou in the PROTON Lab. My PhD research, in collaboration with Sandia National Laboratories, focuses on hardware security, fault analysis and survivable electronics. I also contribute to FACTORY-AI, an NSF-funded project on AI-native industrial IoT networks for smart manufacturing.
