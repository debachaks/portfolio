FundSentinel

An AI approval committee for investment funds that routes itself and proves every decision. A five-agent data team profiles, cleans and documents any fund file with no setup. A seven-agent committee then reviews each fund: a Supervisor routes the work to four specialist reviewers, an Evidence checker demands proof, and a Decision owner rules. AI makes the judgements, while code does the maths, enforces hard rules and verifies every claim against the source data.
