I am a PhD student in the School of Electrical, Computer and Energy Engineering at Arizona State University and a member of the Performance and Resource Optimization in Networks (PROTON Lab), advised by Dr. Eirini Eleni Tsiropoulou. I completed my M.S. in Computer Engineering at ASU and my B.Tech. in Electronics & Communication Engineering from IIIT Guwahati.

I am a researcher with a strong interest in applied machine learning, having worked on EEG-based seizure classification and network optimization using over-the-air techniques. I am now focusing on leveraging machine learning for fault-tolerant and resilient computing systems, bridging my prior experience with future research directions.
